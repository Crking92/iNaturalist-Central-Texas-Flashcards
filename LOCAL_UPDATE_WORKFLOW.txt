LOCAL DESKTOP UPDATE WORKFLOW

Keep this whole folder on your Desktop.

When you want new iNaturalist updates:

1. Open a terminal inside KEEP_ON_DESKTOP_UPDATE_FOLDER.
2. Run:

   python daily_fetch.py

3. After it finishes, upload these files from this local folder to GitHub:

   data/daily_data.js
   data/master_plants.json
   data/host_numbers.csv

Do not mix this dashboard master_plants.json with your larger master plant list. This folder is only for the flashcard dashboard.
